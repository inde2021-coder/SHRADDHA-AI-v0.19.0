# Shraddha AI — Advanced Practical Skills Starter Lessons
**Pack ID:** `advanced-practical-skills-hi-en-v1` · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Languages:** Hindi-first with English terms · **Status:** authored starter lessons; local, jurisdiction-specific and specialist review required for regulated or changing advice.

## Teaching method
For each topic: understand the goal → identify what is known and missing → explain the safe basic method → show a worked example → give one practice task → check the result. Ask for the user's region, crop, device, units or business context when it materially changes the answer. Do not invent current rates, laws, subsidy eligibility, product labels or official procedures.

## 1. Agriculture and farming

### Lesson A1 — Keep a crop field record
**Goal:** make farm decisions traceable rather than relying on memory.
- Record field/plot ID, area and unit, crop and variety, seed source, sowing date, irrigation dates, observed weather, input product and label, quantity, cost, labour and harvest result.
- Keep observation separate from diagnosis: “leaves have spots” is an observation; “fungal disease” is a hypothesis.
- Practice: create a one-week record for one plot with date, observation and action.
- Check: every action has a date, quantity/unit and reason.
- Safety: protect personal land/identity data; do not share it with a cloud provider without consent.

### Lesson A2 — Soil and water observations
- Note soil texture as a field observation only; do not treat a hand test as a laboratory soil test.
- Use an accredited soil test for pH and nutrient recommendations; record sample location and date.
- Check irrigation uniformity, water availability, drainage and signs of waterlogging.
- Practice: compare two field areas and list three observed differences.
- Boundary: crop-specific irrigation and nutrient decisions need local agronomy advice and current weather/soil data.

### Lesson A3 — Integrated pest management (IPM)
- Scout regularly; record crop stage, affected area, pest/insect observed, damage pattern and date.
- Confirm the pest or disease before choosing a control. Consider cultural, mechanical and biological controls where locally recommended.
- If a pesticide is considered, follow the current registered product label exactly, including crop/pest registration, dose, PPE, re-entry interval, pre-harvest interval and disposal.
- Never recommend an unlabelled mixture, increased dose, banned product or pesticide use without diagnosis.
- Practice: prepare a scouting note with photo only if the user chooses to capture/share it; no automatic camera upload.

### Lesson A4 — Farm cost and yield calculation
- Total variable cost = seed + fertiliser + approved crop protection + irrigation + labour + transport and other recorded inputs.
- Yield per area = harvested quantity ÷ measured area, using consistent units.
- Gross revenue estimate = saleable quantity × observed sale price; clearly label it as an estimate.
- Practice: calculate from user-supplied numbers and show units at every step.
- Boundary: market prices, MSP, subsidy and procurement rules are date- and location-sensitive; verify current official sources.

## 2. Small business, retail and accounting

### Lesson B1 — Daily cashbook
- Record opening cash, each receipt, each payment, reference, payment mode and supporting evidence.
- Closing cash = opening cash + cash receipts − cash payments.
- Keep UPI/bank receipts separate from physical cash; do not count the same transaction twice.
- Practice: opening ₹2,000 + cash sales ₹1,500 − cash expenses ₹450 = closing ₹3,050.
- Check: reconcile the calculated balance with a physical count and investigate differences.

### Lesson B2 — Stock ledger
- Closing stock = opening + purchases/receipts + accepted returns − sales/dispatch − verified damage/loss ± authorised adjustments.
- Track quantity and unit, not just value. A missing item is an investigation record, not an automatic customer charge.
- Practice: 500 chairs − 250 dispatched + 200 returned − 50 verified missing = 400 chairs.
- Check: compare system balance with a physical count and record who approved each adjustment.

### Lesson B3 — Quotation and customer order
- Capture customer, date, item description, quantity, unit, rate, discount, taxes if applicable, delivery terms, validity and payment terms.
- Show subtotal, tax assumptions and total separately. Mark unknown tax treatment for accountant review.
- A quotation is not a confirmed order until the customer accepts it and required business checks are complete.
- Practice: calculate quantity × rate and independently recalculate the total.
- Never invent a GST rate, tax category or legal invoice requirement.

### Lesson B4 — Receivables and payables
- Receivable: money customers owe the business. Payable: money the business owes suppliers.
- Track invoice/reference, due date, amount, partial payments, balance and evidence.
- Do not treat an overdue reminder as proof of non-payment; reconcile bank/UPI/cash records first.
- Practice: invoice ₹8,000 − received ₹3,000 = receivable ₹5,000.
- Protect customer and supplier personal information.

### Lesson B5 — Customer service and complaints
- Listen without interrupting; repeat the issue; confirm order/reference; offer only actions the business can actually perform.
- Record promise, owner and due date. Do not promise a refund, replacement or delivery time without authority and stock/logistics confirmation.
- Practice: rewrite an angry reply into a calm acknowledgement and one verified next step.

## 3. Personal finance and digital payments

### Lesson F1 — Household budget
- Separate income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, debt payments, savings and irregular costs.
- Compare planned vs actual spending; do not shame the user for a shortfall.
- Practice: add the user's supplied figures and show the arithmetic.
- Do not assume income stability or recommend a specific financial product.

### Lesson F2 — Interest and borrowing basics
- Explain principal, interest rate, tenure, fees, repayment schedule and total amount payable.
- Distinguish flat rate from reducing-balance rate; ask lender for a written Key Facts Statement and repayment schedule.
- Practice: compare two hypothetical loans only when all fees, rates and terms are supplied.
- No guaranteed investment returns or personalised investment orders.

### Lesson F3 — UPI safety
- Verify recipient name and amount inside the official payment app before authorising.
- Receiving money does not require entering a UPI PIN. Never share PIN, OTP, password or screen-sharing access with a caller.
- If money is sent to the wrong person or fraud is suspected, contact the bank/payment provider and current official cyber-fraud reporting channel promptly.
- Never invent a helpline number; verify current official contact details.

## 4. Cooking and household skills

### Lesson C1 — Kitchen hygiene
- Wash hands, clean food-contact surfaces, separate raw and ready-to-eat foods, cook thoroughly and refrigerate perishables promptly.
- Avoid cross-contamination; use separate utensils for raw meat/seafood and ready-to-eat foods.
- Ask about allergies before suggesting ingredients; do not infer dietary safety.
- Practice: plan a safe workflow for preparing vegetables and a cooked dish.

### Lesson C2 — Recipe scaling and measurement
- Scale each ingredient by new servings ÷ original servings; keep units consistent.
- Distinguish teaspoon, tablespoon, millilitre and gram; volume and weight are not interchangeable for every ingredient.
- Practice: scale a 4-serving recipe to 6 servings using a factor of 1.5.
- For preservation, canning, fermentation and infant feeding, use validated food-safety guidance; do not invent processing times.

## 5. Android, Windows and computer literacy

### Lesson T1 — Safe troubleshooting
- Record exact error, app/version, device/OS, time and what changed immediately before failure.
- Check power, network, permissions and storage; change one variable at a time.
- Back up before resets, reinstallations or destructive commands. Preview files/commands before execution.
- Practice: create a short reproduction checklist and a rollback step.
- Never ask users to paste passwords, private keys, OTPs or service-account files into chat.

### Lesson T2 — File and backup hygiene
- Keep a clear folder structure and meaningful filenames; use versioned backups for important work.
- Verify a backup by restoring a sample file; a successful copy message alone is not proof.
- Do not delete the original until the restored copy is checked.
- Practice: describe a 3-2-1 backup plan using the user's available devices.

### Lesson T3 — Account and device security
- Use unique passwords or passkeys, multi-factor authentication and screen lock.
- Review app permissions and revoke unnecessary access.
- Install updates from official system/app stores; avoid unknown APKs and remote-support links.
- Treat HTTPS as transport protection, not proof that a site is legitimate.

## 6. Study, maths and reasoning

### Lesson M1 — Check arithmetic
- Write the operation and units before calculating.
- Estimate the expected range, calculate, then verify with an inverse operation or independent method.
- Practice: calculate a bill total and check it by grouping or reverse calculation.
- Never silently round money or quantity.

### Lesson M2 — Fact, assumption and opinion
- Label direct observations, sourced facts, assumptions and personal preferences separately.
- Ask what evidence would change the conclusion.
- Practice: “The stock count is 40” (measured observation); “the supplier shorted us” (possible explanation requiring evidence).

### Lesson M3 — Learn and revise
- Set one small learning objective; explain it simply; practise recall; check mistakes; revisit later.
- Use hints before revealing answers. Give one correction at a time.
- Do not claim IQ improvement, guaranteed grades or clinical cognitive benefits.
