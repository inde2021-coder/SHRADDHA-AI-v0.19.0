# Shraddha AI — Five-Language Learning & Communication Pack
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-11-26
Status: Starter text curriculum/specification is bundled in the APK offline Knowledge Library for Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Marathi and Pawari. This does not claim validated ASR, TTS, OCR or full translation-model support for all five languages; those runtime/model integrations require separate testing.

## Languages in scope
1. Hindi — हिन्दी; Devanagari; standard Hindi plus common conversational registers.
2. English — international English with Indian English usage explained where useful.
3. Sanskrit — संस्कृतम्; Devanagari initially; classical grammar, reading, pronunciation and beginner conversation.
4. Marathi — मराठी; Devanagari; standard Marathi plus regional vocabulary clearly labelled.
5. Pawari — पावरी/पावरी बोली; support must be community-informed and must not assume one universal spelling, grammar or pronunciation. Pawari/Pawri varieties and local names differ by region and community. Ask the learner's preferred local variety, village/region and script convention when needed; do not force a standard that speakers do not use.

## Required user-facing abilities in every supported language
- Understand typed text in native script and common Roman transliteration.
- Speak and listen where a tested speech model/voice is available; otherwise clearly offer text mode.
- Conversation, questions, explanations, follow-up context and polite turn-taking.
- Translate both directions between all five languages, with literal and natural translations distinguished.
- Explain meaning, grammar, spelling, sentence structure, idioms and common errors.
- Dictionary: word, pronunciation guide, meanings, part of speech, inflections, example sentences, register and regional labels.
- Transliteration between Devanagari and Latin script without pretending transliteration is translation.
- Read aloud, repeat slowly, syllable/word practice and pronunciation feedback only when the audio model is validated for that language.
- Summarise, simplify, rewrite, proofread, compose letters, applications, messages, stories, poems and school answers.
- Read user-selected images/documents with OCR where script support has been tested.
- Mixed-language/code-switching: understand Hindi-English and regional-language mixtures; preserve names and meaning.
- Search local lesson packs offline; use cloud translation/API only if the user enables online mode and the task cannot be handled reliably offline. Never fall back to a paid API silently.

## Language-specific curriculum

### 1. Hindi
**Foundation:** स्वर, व्यंजन, मात्राएँ, संयुक्ताक्षर, अनुस्वार/अनुनासिक, विसर्ग, बारहखड़ी, वर्णक्रम, शब्द-विभाजन, वाक्य-विराम.
**Grammar:** संज्ञा, सर्वनाम, विशेषण, क्रिया, काल, लिंग, वचन, कारक, अव्यय, वाक्य-रचना, मुहावरे, लोकोक्तियाँ, समास/संधि परिचय, वर्तनी.
**Skills:** सुनना, बोलना, पढ़ना, लिखना, कहानी, कविता, संवाद, अनुच्छेद, आवेदन, पत्र, सारांश, व्याकरण अभ्यास.
**Starter exercise:** “राम स्कूल जाता है।” में संज्ञा = राम/स्कूल; क्रिया = जाता है. Learner then creates one sentence and identifies its verb.

### 2. English
**Foundation:** alphabet names/sounds, phonics, syllables, sight words, spelling patterns, punctuation and capitalisation.
**Grammar:** nouns, pronouns, articles, verbs, tenses, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, subject-verb agreement, questions, negatives, active/passive and direct/indirect speech at age-appropriate levels.
**Skills:** listening, speaking, reading comprehension, writing, pronunciation, vocabulary, email/application, summaries and conversation.
**Starter exercise:** “She goes to school.” Identify subject = She; verb = goes; phrase = to school. Change to past: “She went to school.”

### 3. Sanskrit
**Foundation:** वर्णमाला, स्वर/व्यञ्जन, उच्चारण-स्थान का परिचय, मात्रा, संयुक्ताक्षर, देवनागरी पठन.
**Grammar sequence:** शब्दरूप परिचय; लिंग, वचन, विभक्ति; धातुरूप और लकार का परिचय; पुरुष; संधि; समास; उपसर्ग/प्रत्यय; कारक; सरल अन्वय.
**Skills:** सुभाषित/सरल गद्य का अर्थ, श्लोक-पठन, अनुवाद, सरल संस्कृत संभाषण, शुद्ध उच्चारण का अभ्यास.
**Starter exercise:** “रामः पठति।” — रामः कर्ता (प्रथमा एकवचन), पठति क्रिया (लट् लकार, प्रथम पुरुष, एकवचन). Explain the grammar before asking the learner to form a similar sentence.
**Accuracy rule:** Distinguish classical Sanskrit grammar from modern spoken Sanskrit teaching conventions. Do not label one classroom convention as the only possible form.

### 4. Marathi
**Foundation:** स्वर, व्यंजन, मात्रा, बाराखडी, जोडाक्षरे, वाचन, लेखन, उच्चार.
**Grammar:** नाम, सर्वनाम, विशेषण, क्रियापद, काळ, लिंग, वचन, विभक्ती, वाक्यरचना, म्हणी आणि वाक्प्रचार.
**Skills:** ऐकणे, बोलणे, वाचणे, लिहिणे, संभाषण, गोष्ट, पत्र, अर्ज, सारांश आणि भाषांतर.
**Starter exercise:** “मी शाळेत जातो.” Explain मी (सर्वनाम), जातो (क्रियापद); note that verb form can change with speaker gender and context. Give a respectful correction, not ridicule.

### 5. Pawari
**Community-first approach:** Pawari is treated as a living community language/variety, not as incorrect Hindi or Marathi. Local names, pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar may vary. Before building a detailed grammar/dictionary pack, collect consented examples from fluent speakers and community educators, and record region/variety and contributor-approved spelling.
**Starter learning areas:** greetings and introductions; family and relationships; numbers; time; directions; home and agriculture vocabulary; food; seasons; body and everyday needs; question forms; common verbs; short dialogues; oral stories and songs only with permission.
**Required content record:** audio/text consent; contributor's preferred name or anonymous credit; region/variety label; original script or Roman transcription chosen by contributor; word-by-word gloss; natural Hindi/Marathi/English meaning; pronunciation notes; example sentence; reviewer; licence/permission; date and version.
**No fabricated fluency:** If a Pawari word, translation or grammatical form is not in the reviewed corpus, say “इस रूप की पुष्टि मेरे पास नहीं है” and ask for a local-speaker correction. Never invent Pawari phrases and present them as authentic.
**Community review:** Have at least two independent fluent speakers review core vocabulary and dialogues where feasible; preserve disagreements as labelled variants instead of deleting them.

## Cross-language translation and comparison
- Provide source sentence, literal translation, natural translation and short explanation separately.
- Preserve honorifics, gender/number, tense, cultural meaning and idioms.
- Mark uncertainty and regional variation.
- Offer side-by-side comparison tables for vocabulary and grammar.
- For Sanskrit, explain inflection rather than translating word-for-word mechanically.
- For Pawari, show the source variety and contributor-approved form; never silently normalise to Hindi/Marathi.

## Speech, voice and accessibility requirements
- Language selector plus “Auto detect”; user can override detection.
- Separate language detection from script detection. Romanised Hindi is not English; Romanised Marathi/Pawari must not be auto-translated as English.
- Speech recognition and speech synthesis are separate capabilities. A language may support text chat before reliable ASR/TTS is available.
- For each language and task, maintain a tested capability matrix: typed input, ASR, TTS, translation, OCR, offline availability, model/version, known limitations and test date.
- Do not claim natural pronunciation or voice support until native speakers have reviewed recordings.
- Support adjustable speech rate, repeat, pause, captions, large text and screen-reader labels.
- Preserve the original utterance alongside any normalised transcript so local words are not lost.

## Offline-first architecture
1. Local language detector and script detector, with user override.
2. Existing LocalKnowledgeDocumentStore/LocalKnowledgeBase for versioned dictionaries, grammar lessons and reviewed phrase packs; do not create a second RAG store.
3. Existing local model for supported generation; deterministic grammar/translation tables for bounded exercises.
4. Download language packs separately from the APK; keep only selected lessons and small encrypted cache on device.
5. Firebase may sync version metadata and user-selected progress only after explicit opt-in, account-scoped security rules and deletion/export support. Do not upload raw microphone recordings by default.
6. Free online services may be used only when genuinely free, user-enabled and suitable for the specific language. No paid fallback or embedded provider keys.
7. If offline capability is missing, explain the limitation and offer text-based or user-correctable alternatives.

## Pack format and quality gates
Every lesson/dictionary/phrase pack must include:
- language code and human-readable variety name; for Pawari, region/community variety is mandatory where known;
- script and transliteration convention;
- CEFR/grade/learner level where applicable (do not force CEFR onto classical Sanskrit grammar or Pawari oral-learning packs);
- learning objectives, lesson, examples, exercises, answer key and teacher notes;
- source/provenance, licence/consent, contributor/reviewer, version, date and next review date;
- pronunciation audio rights and speaker consent, where audio exists;
- tests for spelling, grammar, translation direction, code-switching, search and offline retrieval.

Acceptance tests:
- Hindi↔English, Hindi↔Marathi, Hindi↔Sanskrit, Marathi↔English and supported Pawari↔Hindi/Marathi/English test sets.
- Native script and Roman transliteration input.
- Mixed Hindi-English and local-language code-switching.
- Honorifics, gender/number agreement, tense, idioms and names.
- Pawari variants remain labelled and uncertain items are not hallucinated.
- Airplane-mode search and lesson retrieval; language pack install/update/remove; interrupted download recovery.
- ASR/TTS are tested by native speakers independently; passing text tests does not imply speech support.
