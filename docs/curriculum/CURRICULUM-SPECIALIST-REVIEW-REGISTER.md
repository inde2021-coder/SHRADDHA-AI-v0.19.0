## Required sign-off record

Each reviewed lesson must store:
- exact pack ID, lesson ID, content version and SHA-256;
- reviewer name or approved anonymous reviewer ID, qualifications/role and organisation (if the reviewer agrees to attribution);
- scope of review, source references, date and review expiry/recheck date;
- corrections requested and resolution evidence;
- reviewer confirmation that the version reviewed is the version being shipped.

## Subject-specific gates

- **Medical / baby care:** paediatric clinician checks red flags, safe sleep, feeding and emergency advice against current WHO/MoHFW/NHM guidance.
- **Children / learning:** educator checks developmental appropriateness; safeguarding reviewer checks privacy, disclosure and guardian controls; accessibility educator checks alternatives to speech/text-only participation.
- **Gita / Sanskrit:** scholar checks chapter/verse numbering, Sanskrit text variants, transliteration, padaccheda, grammar and glosses. Interpretations from different traditions remain clearly attributed.
- **Pawari:** at least two fluent speakers per target local variety where feasible; contributor consent, spelling choice, audio permission and region labels; disagreements preserved as variants.
- **Agriculture:** local KVK/state agriculture university expert checks crop/pest advice, labels and regional seasonality.
- **Finance / tax / business:** qualified accountant/GST professional checks calculations and current Indian compliance boundaries.
- **Food safety:** qualified food-safety educator checks storage, cooking and preservation statements.
- **Cybersecurity / device safety:** security practitioner checks current platform behaviour and destructive-action safeguards.

## Release rule

No pending pack may be labelled “expert reviewed”, “certified”, “clinically approved” or “official syllabus”. Automated tests validate structure and software behaviour; they do not replace human subject-matter review. A failed or missing review keeps the status pending and the product must present the relevant safety disclaimer.
