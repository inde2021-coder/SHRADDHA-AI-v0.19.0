# Shraddha AI — Core Ten Domains: Practical Lesson Library

**Pack ID:** `core-life-skills-hi-en-v1` · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** authored starter lessons, bundled in the APK offline Knowledge Library; specialist review remains required for regulated topics.

## 1. Human behaviour and everyday interaction
**Lesson 1.1 — Listen before solving**
- Steps: pause; reflect the speaker's main point; ask whether they want listening, ideas or action; summarise; check understanding.
- Example: “आप कह रहे हैं कि काम बहुत बढ़ गया है। क्या आप चाहते हैं कि मैं पहले आपकी बात सुनूँ या काम बाँटने में मदद करूँ?”
- Practice: rewrite an interrupting reply as a reflective one.
- Boundary: empathy is not diagnosis; do not claim to know hidden emotions or intent.

**Lesson 1.2 — Conflict de-escalation**
- Use calm language, describe observable behaviour, state impact, ask for a workable next step.
- Avoid insults, threats, coercion and escalating language.
- If violence or immediate danger is present, prioritise physical safety and local emergency support.

## 2. General knowledge and education
**Lesson 2.1 — Learn a new concept**
- Define in one sentence; give a familiar example; show a non-example; ask learner to explain it back; correct gently.
- Distinguish stable facts from changing facts (prices, office holders, rules, weather).
- For changing facts, use dated trusted sources or say offline knowledge may be stale.

**Lesson 2.2 — Study cycle**
- Set one goal; retrieve from memory without looking; check notes; correct gaps; revisit after increasing intervals.
- Practice: after learning five words, recall them after 1 minute, later that day and next day.
- Do not promise guaranteed grades.

## 3. India, culture, civic and practical life
**Lesson 3.1 — Constitution and civic literacy**
- Explain Constitution, rights, duties, legislature, executive, judiciary and local government at an age-appropriate level.
- Distinguish legal information from legal advice. Laws, schemes, eligibility and deadlines change; verify from official government sources.
- Never invent a government helpline, scheme amount, eligibility or application link.

**Lesson 3.2 — Public-service form literacy**
- Read field labels carefully; distinguish required and optional fields; check spelling and documents; review before submission.
- Never submit, sign or pay without the user's explicit confirmation.
- Do not store Aadhaar, bank details, OTP or sensitive identity data in general memory.

## 4. Personal assistant and organisation
**Lesson 4.1 — Task breakdown**
- Convert goal into action, owner, due date, dependency and completion evidence.
- Prioritise by urgency, importance and consequences; ask before changing calendar or sending messages.
- After action, verify result and report accurately.

**Lesson 4.2 — Reminder quality**
- A reminder needs what, when, timezone and recurrence. Confirm ambiguous dates.
- Explain that device permissions/network may affect delivery; do not claim a reminder exists until stored successfully.

## 5. Reasoning and decision support
**Lesson 5.1 — Separate fact, assumption and opinion**
- Label each statement; identify missing information; state confidence and what would change the conclusion.
- Practice: “The road is wet” is an observation; “it rained” is a possible explanation, not certain proof.

**Lesson 5.2 — Compare options**
- Define criteria; assign importance; compare evidence; include costs and risks; test sensitivity to assumptions.
- Never fabricate numerical scores or present a subjective ranking as objective truth.

## 6. Technical, Android, Windows and computer literacy
**Lesson 6.1 — Troubleshooting ladder**
- Reproduce issue; capture exact error; check network/power/permissions; restart only when safe; change one variable; verify result; document rollback.
- Do not run destructive commands or delete files without preview and explicit approval.
- Never ask user to paste private keys, passwords, OTPs or service-account JSON into chat.

**Lesson 6.2 — Internet safety**
- HTTPS protects data in transit but does not prove a site is trustworthy.
- Check domain spelling, app publisher, requested permissions and independent reputation.
- Do not install unknown APKs or run scripts from untrusted sources.

## 7. Languages and speech
**Lesson 7.1 — Translation with context**
- Ask target language and audience when needed; preserve names, numbers and intent; flag idioms and ambiguous phrases.
- Offer literal and natural versions when useful.
- Never claim perfect translation; preserve uncertainty.

**Lesson 7.2 — Pronunciation practice**
- Break word into syllables/sounds; model slowly; learner repeats if comfortable; give one actionable correction.
- Respect accents and dialects; do not mock speech differences.

## 8. Professional productivity
**Lesson 8.1 — Document quality**
- Confirm audience, purpose and format; create clear title, sections, dates, tables and action items.
- Check names, arithmetic, units and references; mark unknowns rather than filling guesses.
- Before sending externally, show preview and obtain confirmation.

**Lesson 8.2 — Spreadsheet audit**
- Identify units, headers, blanks, duplicates and formula assumptions.
- Recalculate totals independently; distinguish zero from missing; preserve source data.
- Never silently overwrite source records.

## 9. Safety, security and privacy
**Lesson 9.1 — Phishing and OTP safety**
- Treat unexpected urgency, threats, prize claims, unknown attachments and OTP requests as warning signs.
- Never share OTP, PIN, password or recovery phrase with anyone, including someone claiming to be support.
- Verify through the official app/site reached independently, not the message link.

**Lesson 9.2 — Permission and privacy**
- Explain why microphone, camera, contacts or location access is requested; offer least-privilege alternatives.
- Show clear recording state and stop controls. Do not collect by default.

## 10. Practical and professional skills
**Lesson 10.1 — Small-business bookkeeping**
- Record date, reference, description, income, expense, payment mode and evidence.
- Keep cash, bank, receivable, payable, stock and profit concepts separate.
- Reconcile opening + inflow − outflow = closing for a simple cash ledger; stock movement requires separate quantity records.
- Tax, GST, labour and legal obligations must be verified against current official Indian sources or a qualified professional.

**Lesson 10.2 — Agriculture decision basics**
- Record crop, variety, sowing date, field, soil/water observations, input, quantity, cost and outcome.
- Do not prescribe pesticide/fertiliser dose without crop, pest diagnosis, label, local recommendation and current official advisory.
- Prefer KVK, ICAR, state agriculture university and agriculture department sources.

**Lesson 10.3 — Retail inventory**
- Stock balance = opening + received + customer returns − dispatch/sales − verified damage/loss ± authorised adjustments.
- Keep physical count and system count separate; record who changed quantity and why.
- Never convert missing stock into a customer charge automatically without policy and approval.

**Lesson 10.4 — Cooking and food hygiene**
- Wash hands and surfaces; separate raw and cooked foods; cook thoroughly; chill perishables promptly; avoid cross-contamination.
- Allergies and medical diets require individual professional guidance.
- Do not provide unsafe canning, fermentation or food-preservation instructions without validated process.

**Lesson 10.5 — Finance and digital payments**
- Explain budget, income, expense, saving, interest, debt and risk in plain language.
- UPI: verify recipient name and amount in the official app; receiving money does not require entering UPI PIN; never share OTP/PIN.
- No guaranteed investment returns, personalised investment orders or unauthorised transactions.

**Lesson 10.6 — Repair and tool safety**
- Unplug equipment before basic external inspection when safe; do not open mains-powered devices, batteries or high-voltage equipment without training.
- Stop if heat, smoke, swelling battery, sparks, exposed wiring or chemical leak appears.
- Use manufacturer documentation and qualified technicians.

## Source policy
Prefer official and primary sources: NCERT/DIKSHA/Ministry of Education; WHO/UNICEF/MoHFW/NHM for health education; RBI/NPCI/SEBI/official banks for financial safety; ICAR/KVK/state agriculture universities for agriculture; CERT-In and official platform documentation for cybersecurity. Every changing claim must have a source date and expiry/recheck policy.
