# Shraddha AI — Complete Training & Learning Curriculum Catalogue

Date: 2026-11-26
Status: Curriculum scope and structured syllabus specification. Twenty-seven versioned curriculum packs (nine foundational packs plus eighteen Gita chapter packs) are bundled in the APK and indexed into the encrypted offline Knowledge Library at first Chat use. The catalogue is broader than the current lesson corpus; specialist review, additional authored lessons, and separate speech/model integrations remain required.

## Product-wide training rules

- Offline-first: deterministic local skills first, then installed on-device model and verified local knowledge packs. Use an eligible Free API only for genuinely live/online/special tasks when the user enables it and free quota is available. Never silently use a paid API.
- Keep the curriculum modular, versioned and downloadable by subject/age/language. Do not bundle large model weights or a huge corpus into the APK.
- Every pack needs title, age/skill level, language, learning objectives, lesson content, exercises, answer key/rubric, source/provenance, licence, reviewer, version, last-verified date and safety notes.
- Prefer trusted official/public educational sources and licensed/open educational resources. Mark local/state/board alignment only after checking the relevant official syllabus.
- Learning from the user's private chats, microphone, camera, child data or personal documents is off by default. Imported material requires explicit user approval and source/licence checks.
- A tutor must explain uncertainty, accept corrections, avoid shaming and never pretend it has learned a subject when no verified lesson pack/model capability is installed.

## A. The original ten training domains

1. **Human behaviour and everyday interaction** — respectful conversation, listening, empathy, emotional vocabulary, social etiquette, conflict de-escalation, communication across age groups, boundaries and culturally aware interaction. Not a therapist or mind-reader.
2. **General knowledge and education** — school-level and lifelong learning, literacy, numeracy, science, nature, history, geography, arts, study skills, explanations, quizzes and revision.
3. **India, culture, civic and practical life** — Indian geography/history/culture, Constitution and civic literacy, public services, government processes, consumer awareness, road and public safety, local-language awareness. Date-stamp changing rules and ground legal claims in official sources.
4. **Personal assistant and organisation** — notes, tasks, reminders, calendar concepts, routines, planning, prioritisation, summaries, personal knowledge and user-approved preferences. Consequential actions require confirmation and verified results.
5. **Reasoning and decision support** — arithmetic, logic, comparisons, structured problem solving, planning, assumptions, pros/cons, uncertainty, checking work and explaining steps. Do not present guesses as facts.
6. **Technical, Android, Windows and computer literacy** — device settings, files, apps, troubleshooting, networking basics, safe command-line concepts, coding fundamentals, accessibility and paired-PC workflows. Destructive actions require preview and confirmation.
7. **Languages and speech** — Hindi, English and Romanized Hindi initially; grammar, vocabulary, translation, pronunciation, listening and conversation. Add other Indian/world languages only with validated content and tested ASR/TTS packs.
8. **Professional productivity** — writing, email, documents, PDF, spreadsheets, tables, presentations, OCR, data entry, reports, research, workflow planning and file organisation.
9. **Safety, security and privacy** — cyber hygiene, scams, phishing, passwords/passkeys, permissions, backups, data minimisation, consent, child safety, emergency awareness and safe AI use. No offensive cyber instructions.
10. **Practical and professional skills** — business operations, bookkeeping concepts, agriculture, retail, inventory, customer service, entrepreneurship, repair literacy, cooking, finance basics and day-to-day problem solving. Domain-specific advice must be grounded and jurisdiction-aware.

## B. Five additional capability areas

1. **Context and communication-style awareness** — remember user-approved preferences and adapt clarity/detail/tone without hidden sensitive profiling.
2. **Extendable learning library / Skill Factory** — import, verify, version, search, review, activate, disable and roll back approved knowledge packs. No downloaded code execution.
3. **Multimodal understanding** — user-selected images, documents, OCR, screen context and Visual Lens with explicit permissions and privacy controls.
4. **Permissioned task execution** — one canonical tool catalogue; preview → permission/confirmation → execute → verify → action journal. Planning text alone is not task completion.
5. **Self-evaluation and quality improvement** — lesson quizzes, deterministic checks, regression tests, answer-grounding checks, feedback and pack review. Never self-train on private data by default.

## C. Baby Care & Early Childhood Learning (0–6 years)

### C1. Baby Care (0–2 years)
- Feeding education: breastfeeding/formula information, age-appropriate complementary-feeding education when developmentally appropriate, hunger/fullness cues, safe food handling and hydration awareness. Individual feeding/medical concerns go to a qualified paediatric professional.
- Sleep: age-aware safe-sleep education, sleep cues, calming bedtime routine and sleep journal. Always follow current authoritative safe-sleep guidance; never promise prevention of SIDS or replace adult supervision.
- Diaper and hygiene: diaper-changing steps, skin care basics, bathing safety, hand hygiene, laundry and safe environment.
- Growth and development: age-banded milestones, tummy-time/physical play guidance, sensory play, language exposure, social interaction and when to seek professional advice. Milestones are ranges, not diagnosis.
- Health organisation: vaccination schedule reminders linked to official/local health authority information, appointment and symptom notes, questions to ask a clinician. No diagnosis, prescription or medication dosing.
- Baby communication: age-appropriate talking, naming objects, eye-contact/turn-taking when the baby is receptive, responsive interaction, reading aloud, songs and gentle play. Do not force interaction or claim to interpret a baby's thoughts.
- Lullabies and soothing: original or properly licensed lullabies, gentle stories, parent-recorded voice playback only after explicit choice, volume/timer controls and safe listening guidance. No unlicensed commercial songs.
- Parent guidance: practical checklists, caregiver handover notes, routine journal, feeding/diaper/sleep logs and appointment reminders. Logs are guardian-controlled and deletable.
- Safety: choking and burn prevention awareness, safe sleep, bathing supervision, car-seat awareness, poison prevention and emergency escalation education. In an emergency, contact local emergency/medical services; the app is not a medical device.

### C2. Toddler Learning (2–4 years)
- ABC and phonics readiness; Hindi letters/sounds; vocabulary and naming.
- Numbers, counting, shapes, colours, sizes, sorting and simple patterns.
- Animals, fruits, vegetables, body parts, family/community helpers and everyday objects.
- Rhymes, listening, picture books, short stories and story retelling.
- Matching, simple puzzles, pretend play, drawing, music, movement and fine-motor activities.
- Early social-emotional learning: feelings, turn-taking, sharing, asking for help and personal boundaries.

### C3. Kids Academy (4–6 years)
- English and Hindi pre-literacy, phonics, vocabulary, speaking and early reading/writing readiness.
- Mathematics: number sense, counting, comparison, addition/subtraction readiness, shapes, patterns, measurement and time concepts.
- General Knowledge, EVS, nature, environment, basic science observation and curiosity.
- Computer/digital literacy with guardian supervision and screen-safety habits.
- Art, drawing, music, rhythm, storytelling, creativity and life skills.
- School readiness: listening, following simple instructions, communication, independence routines and cooperative play; no high-pressure ranking.

### C4. Abacus course — eight progressive levels
The following is a staged course outline. Exact bead methods, lesson sequence and assessment thresholds must be authored and reviewed by a qualified abacus educator before being marketed as a certified syllabus.

- **Level 1 — Familiarisation:** abacus parts, bead values, finger technique, number recognition and place-value introduction.
- **Level 2 — Single-digit operations:** represent/read numbers; simple addition and subtraction with physical bead movement.
- **Level 3 — Two-digit operations:** tens/ones, carrying/borrowing concepts and guided practice.
- **Level 4 — Multi-digit operations:** larger addition/subtraction, speed with accuracy and mental visualisation foundations.
- **Level 5 — Multiplication foundations:** repeated addition, tables, multiplication representation and practice.
- **Level 6 — Division foundations:** grouping, sharing, division representation and checking answers.
- **Level 7 — Mixed operations:** multi-step problems, mental-abacus imagery, timed practice balanced with accuracy.
- **Level 8 — Mastery and application:** mixed challenge sets, word problems, revision, confidence and individual progress review.
- Every level includes demonstration, guided practice, independent exercise, answer explanation, error correction and a parent/teacher progress summary. No false claims of guaranteed IQ improvement.

## D. Child Learning Tutor (3–10 years)

- Guardian-created profile with age band (3–5, 6–8, 9–10), preferred language, learning level and accessibility preferences; avoid unnecessary child identity data.
- Patient teacher-like loop: ask → wait → assess → hint → simpler explanation → worked example → retry. If correct, explain why, encourage effort and offer a suitable next challenge/review.
- **3–5:** play-based listening, speaking, stories, matching, colours, shapes, counting and pre-literacy.
- **6–8:** foundational reading/writing, Hindi/English vocabulary, arithmetic, simple science, nature and guided problem solving.
- **9–10:** comprehension, multi-step arithmetic, science concepts, reasoning, projects and revision.
- Subjects: Hindi, English, phonics, vocabulary, maths, shapes/patterns, science/nature, environment, GK, creativity, reasoning and social-emotional learning.
- Lesson formats: short explanations, stories, songs, picture/object prompts, drawing, quizzes, worksheets and hands-on offline activities.
- Parent dashboard: lesson history, progress summaries, strengths, concepts to practise, session/break limits, approvals and profile/history deletion.
- Guardian controls are mandatory. No public sharing, targeted ads, purchases, open web browsing, unsupervised external chat, child biometrics/voiceprints or sensitive-data prompts.

## G. Parent/guardian and learner controls

- Guardian consent and age-appropriate profiles; guardian can pause/exit child mode at any time.
- Parent dashboard for approved profiles, lesson packs, progress, journals, schedules, screen/session limits and content approvals.
- Separate child learning progress from adult personal memory. Child data is not used to train models or shared to Firebase by default.
- If cloud sync is offered, it must be explicit opt-in, account-scoped, encrypted, deletable and protected by tested Firebase rules. Keep a small encrypted offline cache.
- No hidden microphone/camera, no raw audio/video upload by default, no face recognition or voiceprint identification in child mode.
- Lullaby/audio playback has volume and timer controls; no continuous playback that interferes with safe sleep guidance.

## H. Curriculum delivery and acceptance gates

1. Convert each domain into small, versioned lesson packs with learning objectives, source/licence and review metadata.
2. Author lesson text, examples, exercises, answer keys and age/language variants. A topic name alone is not training content.
3. Add the packs to the existing LocalKnowledgeDocumentStore/Knowledge pipeline; do not create a second RAG database.
4. Add curriculum browser, download/remove, progress and guardian controls using existing app architecture.
5. Validate factual content with qualified reviewers where required (especially infant care, health, safety and child education).
6. Add deterministic tests for arithmetic/abacus answers, age-band selection, lesson routing, child-safe response policy, offline retrieval and source citations.
7. Test airplane mode, low storage, interrupted downloads, pack rollback, Firebase opt-in/out, account deletion and data isolation.
8. Only claim a course/feature is available after content, UI, runtime integration and tests are complete.
